Sophia Cabral

I'm a computational designer and researcher working on buildings, sensing, robotics, and making. I grew up in Belém, on the edge of the Amazon in northern Brazil, and that connection to natural systems still shapes how I think about the built environment.

My work moves between the studio, the lab, and the construction site. I have taught computational design and worked in a robotics lab at FIU, where I also did my M.Arch; I did my MDes at Harvard, where my research spans several labs and focuses on multi-modal sensing, robotics, and materials. I have built tech-and-art installations, and I am co-founder and CTO of FixAI, which builds AI tools for people who maintain existing buildings.

In industry, I have worked as a computational designer at ArquitectonicaGEO, as a Robotic Multi-Modal Researcher at Autodesk, and with the U.S. Department of State's Bureau of Overseas Buildings Operations (OBO), Office of Building and Construction, building digital tools to track progress and monitor a construction site in Mauritius. Whether teaching, designing, or coding, my goal is the same: technology and buildings that care for the people and the living systems around them.
